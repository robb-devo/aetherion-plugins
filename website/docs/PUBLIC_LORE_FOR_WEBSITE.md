# Aetherion — Public lore brief (website only)

Condensed from the Story Bible for **marketing / atmosphere**.  
Keep this in the back of your head. **Do not plaster the site with lore walls.**

Tone on the site: myth, harbour, bells, islands, skills, dungeons — a world that remembers.  
Tease. Don’t explain the ending.

---

## What the public already “knows”

- This place was once **Eldervale** — a harbour realm in the sky, tied to other worlds by anchor chains.
- Something broke. The name people say now is **Aetherion**.
- Every morning and evening, **bells** ring across the world. Nobody agrees who rings them. Children get a scary bedtime story about a mad king. That’s enough.
- Players arrive at **Anker Harbour**, sail to skill islands (**Eldervale** outer isles), mine, farm, forage, fish, run **dungeons**, face bosses. The MMO loop is the front door; the myth is the weather.

## Mood words (use sparingly in copy)

Harbour · anchors · bells · ash · gold light · mist · islands · the fade (as rumour, not lecture) · a name that replaced another

## Hard spoilers — never on the public site

Do **not** put these on landing **or** wiki Lore pages:

- The king’s true name arc spelled out as fact for marketing
- Maelis / Elin tragedy details, Helios heart, Seraphine’s full truth
- The Unbroken / Nihil / World Eater endgame
- “He rings the bells to hold the world together” as a stated fact
- Any Act III reveal

If you write a Chronicle teaser: **legend and rumour only.** Wrong or incomplete is fine. NPCs don’t know everything — neither should the homepage.

## How to use this on the site

- Hero / finale: one sharp myth-tinged line max, then Play.
- World section / wiki World: place names + feeling (Harbour, Eldervale, mines, Throne of Ashes) — screenshots do the heavy lifting.
- **Wiki Lore (Phase 2):** short rumour pages OK — village tales, bells mystery, old name Eldervale. Still no timeline dump, no character sheets, no “five layers of truth.”
- Nowhere else: paste the Story Bible.
